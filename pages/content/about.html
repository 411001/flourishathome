Biography

My career in social work began more than thirty-five years ago with the Inner London Probation Service in the U.K. After several years as a probation officer, I moved to the United States and joined the Massachusetts Department of Social Services, where I worked as both a social worker and supervisor. After a brief career break, I returned to social work and worked for home care and hospice agencies, and most recently worked in a Continuing Care Retirement Community. For the past seventeen years, my work has focused primarily on supporting older adults in the community.

As a social worker, my practice is guided by the profession's core values and ethical principles: service, social justice, dignity and worth of the person, the importance of human relationships, integrity, and competence. I place particular importance on confidentiality, privacy, and each person's right to make their own decisions.

Community matters to me, too. I regularly volunteer at St. Irene's Church and serve on the Cemetery Committee in Carlisle, Massachusetts.

Flourish At Home grew quite naturally from my experience as a social worker—and from the fact that I genuinely enjoy many of the things other people would happily avoid. I like cooking, gardening, organizing, and keeping things tidy. A cluttered cupboard presents less of a problem than an invitation. These everyday pleasures have become part of the practical, non-medical support I offer.

I also believe strongly in mentoring young adults and giving them opportunities to develop their talents. Younger members of our community will contribute to the Flourish At Home website and monthly newsletters. It seems only fitting: a business devoted to helping one generation flourish should make a little room for the next one, too.

— Maeve Lobo, LICSW
